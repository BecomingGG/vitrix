Hands on, on purpose

VITRIX simulation is scenario-based training. A case unfolds. The monitor changes. The team has to assign roles, speak clearly, and act before the window closes.

Faculty design the scenario, run the room, and debrief what the team actually did. The point is not a perfect script. The point is a sharper next attempt — skills, confidence, and better outcomes.
