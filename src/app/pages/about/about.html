About VITRIX

An education and simulation institute for emergency medicine, critical care, and human performance. Built for the critical moment.

Train for reality

VITRIX is a modern education and simulation institute focused on emergency medicine, critical care and human performance.

We bridge knowledge, hands-on training and real-world experience to prepare healthcare professionals for the moments that matter. Guidelines set the standard. Simulation makes the standard usable when the room gets loud.

Based in Tbilisi, with a global vision: a more prepared healthcare community, and a safer tomorrow.
